# Add Flow — onboard-to-offboard

## What ran

`add-flow` checklist (followed by hand) for a new small-business banking flow, built to back the `bank_core` workshop dataset in `solid-garbanzo-2026` (session 18, "Stop waiting on the analyst"). Seven tables: `branches`, `tariff_plans`, `clients`, `accounts`, `plan_changes`, `monthly_activity`, `loans`. `n_samples` is the number of clients; at 3,000 clients the flow makes ~3.6k accounts, ~450 plan changes, ~66k activity rows, and ~740 loans in about half a second.

## What changed

- **Enterprise brief:** `enterprise_dataflow_briefs/onboard-to-offboard.md`, added to that folder's `CLAUDE.md` imports
- **Scrutiny brief:** `scrutinize/specific_briefs/onboard-to-offboard.md` (12 checks)
- **Field generator:** `field_generators/kz_business.py` (Kazakh-style legal names, BINs, IBANs under fictional bank code 999)
- **Generator package:** `record_generators/onboard_to_offboard/`. Accounts are simulated month by month per client so closures, plan changes, dormancy, and partial exits share one timeline. Business events: BUSINESS fee 4,900 → 8,900 KZT on 2025-03-01 (closures 2× for four months, 9× in Karaganda, plus downgrades to START), SMART plan launch on 2025-12-01
- **IDs are serial** (`CL-000001`, `AC-0000001`) rather than UUIDs, so they stay identical across runs; `identifiers.generate_n_random_uuids` bypasses the seeded stream
- **Registered** in `inject_into_pg.py` as `O2O`
- **BRIEFS.md:** new `has dataset` row
- **Tests:** `tests/adorable_thunder/make/record_generators/test_onboard_to_offboard.py` (11 invariants: reproducibility, date chain, closure fields, activity window, plan chain, SMART timing, fee-increase signal, dormancy, partial exits, loan status)

## Verification

- `GeneratorConfig(n_samples=3000).make()` checked by hand: KZT 87% / USD 9% / EUR 4%; ~0.9% of accounts close per month outside the fee window and ~1.8% inside it; Karaganda closures roughly double in Apr–Jul 2025; ~10% of open accounts dormant; 20% of clients fully exited over two years; median balance micro 0.28M / small 2.3M / medium 17M KZT
- ruff and pyright (strict) clean on the new and changed files; the repo's other pre-existing ruff findings are untouched
- **Not run:** the Postgres inject (step 5). Docker was not running, so `inject_into_pg --flow onboard_to_offboard` is untested against a live database
